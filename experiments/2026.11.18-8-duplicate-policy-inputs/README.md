# Duplicate policy inputs

Measure whether deduplicating selected training positions could free a material share of policy updates. Among 318,400 paid positions, 310,629 encoded inputs were distinct. Removing every repeated input would free at most 7,771 slots per epoch (2.4%). Keys included the piece placement, side to move, castling rights, en passant square, halfmove clock and repetition count.

Deferred training because the opportunity was small.
